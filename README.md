# NVIDIA Financial Statement Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes NVIDIA Corporation's financial performance over three financial years (FY2024–FY2026) using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

The project focuses on financial statement analysis, profitability, liquidity, operating efficiency, leverage, interest coverage, and cash flow analysis. An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to present the results in a clear and management-friendly format.

## Objectives

- Analyze NVIDIA's revenue and profit growth.
- Evaluate profitability trends.
- Assess the company's liquidity position.
- Analyze working-capital and asset efficiency.
- Evaluate leverage and interest coverage.
- Analyze operating, investing, and financing cash flows.
- Build an interactive financial statement analysis dashboard.

## Key Findings

### 1. Strong Revenue and Profit Growth

NVIDIA's revenue increased from $60.9 billion in FY2024 to $130.5 billion in FY2025 and $215.9 billion in FY2026. Net income increased from $29.8 billion to $72.9 billion and then $120.1 billion over the same period.

### 2. Strong Profitability

Net margin increased from 48.86% in FY2024 to 55.85% in FY2025 and remained high at 55.61% in FY2026. ROCE increased from 59.84% in FY2024 to 87.07% in FY2025 before moderating to 74.67% in FY2026.

### 3. Strong Liquidity Position

The current ratio increased from 4.17 in FY2024 to 4.44 in FY2025 before declining to 3.90 in FY2026. The quick ratio followed a similar pattern, moving from 3.67 to 3.88 and then 3.24.

### 4. Efficiency Needs Monitoring

Inventory days increased from 85.93 days in FY2025 to 91.96 days in FY2026, while receivable days increased from 46.25 days to 52.01 days. Asset turnover declined from 1.47x to 1.36x.

### 5. Low Leverage and Strong Interest Coverage

Gearing declined from 22.59% in FY2024 to 10.67% in FY2025 and 5.38% in FY2026. Interest cover increased from 128.30x to 329.77x and then 503.43x.

### 6. Strong Operating Cash Flow Growth

Operating cash flow increased from $28.1 billion in FY2024 to $64.1 billion in FY2025 and $102.7 billion in FY2026. Operating cash flow remained positive throughout the three-year period.
